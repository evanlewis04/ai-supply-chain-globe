# GlobalWafers Hsinchu (300mm wafer plant & HQ)

GlobalWafers — the world's third-largest silicon wafer maker — runs its
headquarters and a 300mm/200mm polished and epitaxial wafer plant at
No. 2 Creation Road 1, Hsinchu Science Park, about 100 km up the west
coast from TSMC's Fab 18 in Tainan. It is one of TSMC's six publicly
identified main wafer suppliers, and it stands here for the wafer/ingot
stage of the chain: the step that consumes Spruce Pine quartz crucibles
and feeds blank 300mm wafers into leading-edge fabs.

Coordinates are the Hsinchu city centroid (precision `city`); the street
address is published but has not been independently geocoded to site
precision.

Capacity is `null`: GlobalWafers reports revenue and wafer-area
shipments company-wide, not per-site wafer starts.

**Reviewer notes:** (1) TSMC does not disclose supplier shares; the
"main wafer suppliers" list is trade-press reporting (Opportimes 2021)
consistent with later coverage — treat the GlobalWafers→TSMC link as
well-established but unquantified. (2) Shin-Etsu and SUMCO are larger
suppliers globally; GlobalWafers was chosen as the representative wafer
node because it is Taiwanese (geographic proximity to Fab 18) and
publicly listed (6488 on TPEx, for the finance overlay). Adding
Shin-Etsu Shirakawa later would also serve the photoresist story.
