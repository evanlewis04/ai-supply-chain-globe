# MidAmerican Energy (Iowa service territory)

The utility serving Microsoft's West Des Moines data center cluster.
Iowa's cheap, abundant wind generation (roughly a third of state
generation, with MidAmerican the dominant builder) is a major reason the
GPT-4 training campus exists where it does — the clearest example in the
v1 slice of energy availability determining AI geography.
